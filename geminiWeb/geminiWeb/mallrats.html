About Us

The Mallrats is part of the Eastern Pennsylvania Robotics Alliance (EPRA), a nonprofit organization that competes in FIRST robotics competitions. FIRST is an international organization that organizes robotics competitions for kids grades k-12 where they learn how to engineer and collaborate to solve problems. The Mallrats competes in the FIRST Technical Challenge (FTC) section of the FIRST organization. FTC is where kids grade 7-12 build 18" by 18" by 18" robots made of aluminum, plastic, and other materials that they build and code themselves to compete with and against other teams to complete tasks to earn points in competitions. The Mallrats has been competing in FTC since 2020, and since then the team members have built awesome robots, had a lot of fun, and advanced to states.

Peri?

Season: 2024 - 25

GitHub Repository: Swerve Lyra Code
