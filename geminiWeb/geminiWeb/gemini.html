History

Pre-FRC Era

Erin McCullough used to be a part of a robotics team at her middle school, but she wasn't able to be as involved as the boys on the team because she was a girl. Because of this she went to her father, Joe McCullough, and asked to start a Girl Scout robotics team. Mr. Joe created 2 FLL teams in 2011 (need to check that year) that were associated with the Girl Scouts of Eastern Pennsylvania.

Early Tin Mints

When Erin aged out of FLL in 2013, they created The Tin Mints, FRC team 4575, that was the first Girl Scout FRC team in the district. We started out strong with 4 people on the team, and the team ranked 24th in the whole district, getting many rookie and non-rookie awards. We also had some help from our mentor team called Royal Assault, FRC 357, based out of Upper Darby High School.

In 2020, 4575 Competed at the Week 1 district event at Hatboro-Horsham high school

Covid Years

After Week 1, every competition for the rest of the season was canceled 4575 was located in a mall, and could not access their shop regardless of safety precautions taken. All FLL and FRC teams continued to meet virtually. During 2020, 4575 also created FTC 18387, The Mall Rats, who competed virtually during their first year. When it became safe, 4575 reopened their doors both to normal programs and summer camp.

In 2023, FRC 4575 and FRC 6327 combined into one team under the unified name Gemini. 4575 is now a co-ed team.

Awards

In 2015, during Recycle Rush, we went to the Queen City Regional, and were the second pick of the winning alliance with Team Titanium and The Pink Team. In 2017, at the Montgomery District Event, we won the Chairman's award and competed for the award at the District Championship in Lehigh, PA. We won this award, in part, for creating FRC 6327, The Tin Men 6327 would later change their name to The Tin Soldiers, and then The Fighting Gophers Additionally, 4575 created the Eastern PA Robotics Alliance, a 501(c)3 non-profit that supported many FLL and FLL jr. teams, as well as the 2 FRC teams. In 2016, we ran a 2 week long summer camp for the first time with over 40 students. Our summer camp continues to be run every year up to the present. Click Here for more Info About summer camp
